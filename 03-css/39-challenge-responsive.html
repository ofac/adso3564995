Explore Hyrule

At the heart of this majestic kingdom lies Hyrule Field, a vast grassland that leads to diverse locations: bustling settlements, an awe-inspiring volcano, a picturesque lake, an unforgiving desert, and other interesting spots. To cover long distances on foot, you can make use of the new dash ability.

Furthermore, with the simple press of a button, you will be able to clear gaps and obstacles. You will even befriend a faithful mare, whom you can ride once you have progressed in your adventure.

Wisdom is the key

Thoroughly explore every corner of the kingdom and lend a hand to the inhabitants with their troubles, as your goodwill will be rewarded. No matter where you are, time will pass without pause. After sunset, you might encounter new faces... and enemies.

If you are ever unsure of where to go, take a look at the chronology to review past events, check your objectives, and marvel at a tapestry that recounts Link's journey.
